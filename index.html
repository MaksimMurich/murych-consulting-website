How we work

You stay in control the whole way.

No rip-and-replace, no new software for your drivers, no credentials handed around. You connect us from your own account, and you can disconnect us from the same place.

01

You authorize

From inside your own platform account, you grant read-only access. The credentials stay yours, and you can revoke access at any moment without asking us.

02

We consolidate

We read your records — trips, utilization, HOS, inspections, fuel and card spend — and reconcile them into one clean, current dataset.

03

You get the reporting

Cost, utilization, compliance and settlement reporting in a single dashboard, kept up to date automatically, visible to you and your back office.
